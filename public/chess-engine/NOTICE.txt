Custom Saras engine
I trained a move-selection model on my public Chess.com games to learn my opening
choices and the kinds of moves I tend to make. Stockfish 19 Lite’s NNUE neural
network evaluates positions. My model uses those evaluations and my learned
preferences to choose moves, with a 1,750 Elo search as its strength baseline.
It looks for sound sacrifices when the position allows them.
